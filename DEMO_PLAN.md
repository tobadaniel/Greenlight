# Greenlight — demo video plan

Format rules (standing): narrated voiceover, motion and sound effects
throughout, the app framed on a branded landscape canvas at 1280x720 —
never a raw full-screen capture. Target length 2:15–2:45. Judges judge
the demo, the README and the video first, so the score journey is the
hero shot.

IMPORTANT: the recorded run must be a LIVE run (real SERV + real Reppo
verdicts, badge reading LIVE). Demo mode is for exploring the app, not
for the submitted video. If live scores differ from the seeded demo
scores (38 → 71 → 92), narrate the real numbers — never restate the
simulated ones over a live recording.

## Shot list + narration script

Canvas: dark green branded frame, "GREENLIGHT" wordmark top-left,
track line bottom: "serv hackathon edition 02 · reppo track".

1. (0:00–0:12) Cold open on the flawed support draft, zoom on the words
   "full cash refund".
   VO: "this support reply was written by an ai agent. it promises a
   full cash refund. the company's policy does not offer cash refunds
   at all. greenlight exists to catch this before a customer ever
   sees it."
   SFX: soft warning tone on "does not offer".

2. (0:12–0:30) Pull back to the full app. Cursor picks the support case,
   clicks Run the gate.
   VO: "greenlight is a quality gate for agent output. serv reasoning
   reads the draft against the brief and pulls out every checkable
   claim. then reppo's evaluator panel scores it."

3. (0:30–0:55) Round 1 verdict lands: BLOCK, claims list shows the
   unsupported promises in red.
   VO: "round one: blocked. the panel found the invented refund, the
   three day payout that does not exist, the personal return
   extension, and the 24/7 support claim. four promises the company
   cannot keep."
   SFX: low block buzz.

4. (0:55–1:25) Rewrite happens, round 2 verdict: REVIEW, score up.
   VO: "serv rewrites the draft from the panel's own critique, and the
   loop runs again. the refund is gone, store credit is stated
   correctly, but the reply still gives the customer no next step, so
   it stays at review."
   Motion: score chart line animates up.

5. (1:25–1:50) Round 3: SHIP. Green light panel, traffic-light dots in
   the header flip to green.
   VO: "round three: greenlit. every statement now traces back to the
   brief, and the customer gets one clear next step. that score
   journey is the whole point: feedback you can measure."
   SFX: greenlight chime.

6. (1:50–2:15) Fast cuts: listing case (48 hours, IP68, $59 struck
   through by verdicts), outreach case (312% ROI blocked).
   VO: "it works on anything an agent writes. listings that invent
   specs. outreach emails built on roi figures nobody measured.
   blocked, rewritten, and only shipped when they pass."

7. (2:15–2:40) Close on the audit trail scrolling, then the wordmark.
   VO: "every step is on the record: what was checked, what the
   verdict was, what changed. greenlight. nothing ships until it
   passes."
   SFX: chime out.

## Production notes

- Record the app at 1440x1000 in the local server, live mode, case 1.
- Reppo verdicts take 30–90s per round live: record full runs, then
  time-remap the waiting stretches to ~2s with a subtle "waiting for
  the evaluator panel" pulse so the video stays honest about the wait
  without showing it in real time.
- Narration: same voice pipeline as previous demos (clone-free,
  assumed voice), normalised to -14 LUFS.
- Export 1280x720, H.264, plus a vertical crop is NOT needed.
